Idea validation canvas

This is a deliberately small public prototype. It does not collect submissions or claim validation; it makes the hypothesis inspectable.

Who this is for

Solo founders and knowledge workers

The test

Launch a public, evidence-led landing page that offers a 10-minute self-diagnostic for identifying their most urgent daily tasks with a simple prompt and a green bar progress tracker

What would count

The prototype completes five simulated tasks in under 10 minutes and generates a weekly summary card with the statement 'You completed 80% of your tasks today. You spent 10 minutes. You did not skip any.'

Current evidence: No actual user data or real-time task logs have been collected; the evidence is derived from an internal simulation, not from real-world behavior
