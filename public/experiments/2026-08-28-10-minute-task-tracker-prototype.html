Idea validation canvas

This is a deliberately small public prototype. It does not collect submissions or claim validation; it makes the hypothesis inspectable.

Who this is for

Solo founders who struggle with daily task planning and time management

The test

A public, evidence-led prototype that helps founders plan and track daily tasks in under 10 minutes with no login or device requirements

What would count

The prototype successfully simulates a user completing five tasks under two minutes each, with a green bar that fills and generates a weekly summary card after five tasks

Current evidence: No real-world user data or actual task logs were observed; the test relied solely on internal simulation
