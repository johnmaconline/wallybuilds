Idea validation canvas

This is a deliberately small public prototype. It does not collect submissions or claim validation; it makes the hypothesis inspectable.

Who this is for

Solo founders who struggle with daily task tracking and morning routines

The test

A 10-minute self-check tool that prompts users to list five tasks under two minutes each, tracks completion with a growing green bar, and generates a weekly summary card showing completion percentage and time spent.

What would count

The tool produces a consistent, accurate summary card after five tasks are completed in under ten minutes, with no errors or crashes in simulation.

Current evidence: No real-world user input, task logs, or actual task completion data has been observed. The prototype runs in simulation only, and no one has used it in practice.
