Idea validation canvas

This is a deliberately small public prototype. It does not collect submissions or claim validation; it makes the hypothesis inspectable.

Who this is for

Solo founders who struggle with daily task prioritization and morning routines

The test

A public, text-based 'Morning Routine Audit' tool that prompts users to list three morning tasks under two minutes each, then displays a visual progress bar and a daily summary card stating their completion rate and total time spent

What would count

The tool generates a valid summary card after three tasks are entered, with the progress bar filling appropriately and the completion percentage calculated accurately

Current evidence: No actual user interactions or real-world deployments have occurred; the test is based on internal simulation and does not include observed user behavior or engagement
