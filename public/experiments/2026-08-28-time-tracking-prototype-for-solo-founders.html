Idea validation canvas

This is a deliberately small public prototype. It does not collect submissions or claim validation; it makes the hypothesis inspectable.

Who this is for

Solo founders who struggle with daily workflow inefficiencies

The test

Create a 10-minute time-tracking prototype that visualizes task completion with real-time feedback and a weekly summary card

What would count

The prototype runs without errors and generates a weekly summary card for a simulated user workflow of 5 tasks over 10 minutes

Current evidence: No public evidence of actual user flow data or task completion patterns in real-world workflows
