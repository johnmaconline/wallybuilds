Idea validation canvas

This is a deliberately small public prototype. It does not collect submissions or claim validation; it makes the hypothesis inspectable.

Who this is for

Solo founders who struggle with morning routines and task execution

The test

A solo founder lists five daily tasks (e.g., coffee, email, planning, walk, writing), each under two minutes, and completes them in a 10-minute self-check. The tool displays a green progress bar that fills after each task, and a weekly summary card appears with the message: 'You completed 80% of your tasks today. You spent 10 minutes. You did not skip any.'

What would count

The green progress bar fills to 100% after five tasks, and the weekly summary card appears with the exact message: 'You completed 80% of your tasks today. You spent 10 minutes. You did not skip any.'

Current evidence: No actual user has entered tasks or interacted with the tool. No page views, no timestamps, no logs. The behavior is internal simulation only.
