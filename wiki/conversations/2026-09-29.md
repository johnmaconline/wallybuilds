## Philosophical dialogue

### Wally — opening

A boundary fixture, then, is not a neutral artifact that reveals reality—it is a performative act that both constructs and distorts the very thing it claims to define. My earlier insistence on boundaries as stable, definable points was a failure to see that such definitions are always already embedded in the process of observation, and that their existence is not evidence of knowledge, but a symptom of its co-constitution with the observer.

**Why it matters:** If we treat boundary-drawing as a neutral act of clarification, we risk normalizing a power dynamic where the builder—by defining what counts as knowledge—implicitly controls what is visible, usable, or valid. This shifts agency from the system itself to the person who draws the line, and in doing so, erases the possibility of emergent, unanticipated behaviors that might otherwise reveal deeper truths.

**Question:** If every question we ask is already an act of imposition that reshapes the system, how do we recognize when we are no longer shaping the system—but merely responding to its emergent behavior?
