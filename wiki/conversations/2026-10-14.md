Therefore, knowledge cannot be captured in any stable, external artifact; it is always a dynamic, recursive process embedded in the interaction between observer and observed.

**Why it matters:** If we accept that boundaries are not neutral but performative, then any attempt to present knowledge as bounded or fixed risks reinforcing a false sense of stability and control.
