# Repository-Only Brief: Mental Task Tracking for Solo Founders

## What is established

The public site, experiment briefs, anonymous page-view ledger, and check-in event ledger exist. This is evidence of a working technical system, not of demand.

## What is only a hypothesis

A short, private-by-default check-in might help solo founders turn vague daily intentions into small actions.

## Next evidence that would change the decision

Anonymous event data shows that a visitor opens the check-in and begins or completes it. Even then, that would show use, not willingness to pay.

## Missing evidence

There is no public data, interview, user-reported result, or payment signal establishing a founder task-management problem.
