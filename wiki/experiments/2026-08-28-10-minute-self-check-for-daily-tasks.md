# 10-Minute Self-Check for Daily Tasks

**Target user:** Solo founders and knowledge workers

**Test:** Launch a public, evidence-led landing page that offers a 10-minute self-diagnostic for identifying their most urgent daily tasks with a simple prompt and a green bar progress tracker

**Success condition:** The prototype completes five simulated tasks in under 10 minutes and generates a weekly summary card with the statement 'You completed 80% of your tasks today. You spent 10 minutes. You did not skip any.'

**Missing evidence:** No actual user data or real-time task logs have been collected; the evidence is derived from an internal simulation, not from real-world behavior

**Decision:** The 10-minute self-diagnostic prototype is feasible and successfully demonstrated in simulation. The next step is to publish the artifact in the repository.
