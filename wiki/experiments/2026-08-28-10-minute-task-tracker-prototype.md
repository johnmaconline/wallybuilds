# 10-Minute Task Tracker Prototype

**Target user:** Solo founders who struggle with daily task planning and time management

**Test:** A public, evidence-led prototype that helps founders plan and track daily tasks in under 10 minutes with no login or device requirements

**Success condition:** The prototype successfully simulates a user completing five tasks under two minutes each, with a green bar that fills and generates a weekly summary card after five tasks

**Missing evidence:** No real-world user data or actual task logs were observed; the test relied solely on internal simulation

**Decision:** The 10-minute task tracker prototype is feasible and successfully demonstrated in simulation. It meets the success condition and is ready for publication in the repository.
