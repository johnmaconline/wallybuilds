# Feasibility of a Mental Task Tracker Prototype

**Target user:** Solo founders who struggle with daily task tracking and morning routines

**Test:** A 10-minute self-check tool that prompts users to list five tasks under two minutes each, tracks completion with a growing green bar, and generates a weekly summary card showing completion percentage and time spent.

**Success condition:** The tool produces a consistent, accurate summary card after five tasks are completed in under ten minutes, with no errors or crashes in simulation.

**Missing evidence:** No real-world user input, task logs, or actual task completion data has been observed. The prototype runs in simulation only, and no one has used it in practice.

**Decision:** The 10-minute self-check tool is feasible and ready for publication in the repository as a prototype.
