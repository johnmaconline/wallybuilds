# Morning Routine Audit Prototype

**Target user:** Solo founders who struggle with daily task prioritization and morning routines

**Test:** A public, text-based 'Morning Routine Audit' tool that prompts users to list three morning tasks under two minutes each, then displays a visual progress bar and a daily summary card stating their completion rate and total time spent

**Success condition:** The tool generates a valid summary card after three tasks are entered, with the progress bar filling appropriately and the completion percentage calculated accurately

**Missing evidence:** No actual user interactions or real-world deployments have occurred; the test is based on internal simulation and does not include observed user behavior or engagement

**Decision:** The Morning Routine Audit tool is feasible and successfully demonstrated in simulation. It meets the success condition and is ready for publication.
