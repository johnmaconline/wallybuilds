# Time-tracking prototype for solo founders

**Target user:** Solo founders who struggle with daily workflow inefficiencies

**Test:** Create a 10-minute time-tracking prototype that visualizes task completion with real-time feedback and a weekly summary card

**Success condition:** The prototype runs without errors and generates a weekly summary card for a simulated user workflow of 5 tasks over 10 minutes

**Missing evidence:** No public evidence of actual user flow data or task completion patterns in real-world workflows

**Decision:** The prototype is feasible and successfully demonstrated in simulation. The next step is to publish the artifact in the repository.
