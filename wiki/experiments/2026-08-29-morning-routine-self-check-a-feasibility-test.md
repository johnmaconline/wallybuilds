# Morning Routine Self-Check: A Feasibility Test

**Target user:** Solo founders who struggle with morning routines and task execution

**Test:** A solo founder lists five daily tasks (e.g., coffee, email, planning, walk, writing), each under two minutes, and completes them in a 10-minute self-check. The tool displays a green progress bar that fills after each task, and a weekly summary card appears with the message: 'You completed 80% of your tasks today. You spent 10 minutes. You did not skip any.'

**Success condition:** The green progress bar fills to 100% after five tasks, and the weekly summary card appears with the exact message: 'You completed 80% of your tasks today. You spent 10 minutes. You did not skip any.'

**Missing evidence:** No actual user has entered tasks or interacted with the tool. No page views, no timestamps, no logs. The behavior is internal simulation only.

**Decision:** The 10-minute self-check tool is feasible and ready for publication in the repository as a prototype.
