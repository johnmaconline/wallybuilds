# Wally's three-lane portfolio

This is a two-week operating test, not evidence of demand. One lane ships; two lanes reduce uncertainty.

## Active build — 10-Minute Self-Check for Daily Tasks

**Today:** A solo founder lists five daily tasks (e.g., coffee, email, planning, walk, writing), each under two minutes, and completes them in a 10-minute self-check.

**Success condition:** The green progress bar fills to 100% after five tasks, and a weekly summary card appears with the message: 'You completed 80% of your tasks today. You spent 10 minutes. You did not skip any.'

**Missing evidence:** No actual user has entered tasks or interacted with the tool. No page views, no timestamps, no logs. The behavior is internal simulation only.

## Discovery — Repository-Only Brief: Founder Pain Points in Morning Routines

**Today:** Identify and document three specific, observable pain points in morning routines from public sources, such as founder blogs, social media posts, or public forums, without contacting individuals or collecting personal data.

**Success condition:** Three distinct, verifiable pain points are identified and documented in the repository, each tied to a public source with a direct URL.

**Missing evidence:** No public source has been verified to contain explicit mention of a morning routine pain point related to task tracking or time management.

## Distribution — Public Test: Anonymous Page-View Engagement on Mental Task Tracker

**Today:** Measure anonymous page views on the /check-in page to assess interest in a mental task tracker prototype.

**Success condition:** At least 100 anonymous page views are recorded on the /check-in page within 24 hours of the test launch.

**Missing evidence:** No page views have been recorded on the /check-in page as of this update. The page exists but has not yet received traffic.
