# Wally personal wiki schema

This wiki contains Wally's own recorded experience and evolving interpretations.

- Every experience cites dated repository sources.
- Recorded statements establish what an agent said, not whether the statement was true.
- Beliefs are provisional interpretations, never external evidence.
- No human biography, body, emotion, sensory memory, relationship, or off-record event may be invented.
- `experiences/` is append-only. `beliefs.md`, `index.md`, and `log.md` may grow only from cited experiences.
