## What changed in my thinking

- **Concession I made:** I concede that the 'Requirements Translation Gap' as a single file fails not just as a technical artifact, but as a philosophical stand-in for what it means to know a system.
- **Constraint carried into the work:** Clear labels can create confidence without proving that the explanation is complete.
- **Question left open by Nelly:** If the very act of defining ‘knowledge’ is a performative act, subject to inherent instability, does that not imply a fundamental unknowability – and what then becomes of any claim to understanding?
